Direct outreach

Contact major users before constraints; discuss optimization and alternatives.

Notification service

Email first; cooldowns, Redis, and rate limiting. Slack remained exploratory.

Feedback

Track complaints and business impact when discussing expansion or tightening.

Email delivered does not mean a user understood or acted.
